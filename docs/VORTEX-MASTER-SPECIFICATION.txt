VORTEX by EVORIAN / OMNIA
MASTER SPECIFICATION

PRODUCT
VORTEX is a cinematic discovery and catalog platform for movies and series. TMDB is a metadata/catalog source; TMDB data does not itself grant streaming rights.

MEDIA MODEL
Movie fields: id, tmdb_id, title, original_title, overview, poster_path, backdrop_path, rating, release_date, duration, genres, certification, director, cast, origin_country, original_language, streaming_sources.
Series extends movie data with seasons_count, total_episodes and seasons. Episodes contain id, season_number, episode_number, title, overview, duration, air_date, still_path and watch_progress.

PLAYER
Controls include play/pause, +/-10 seconds, volume, subtitles/audio/quality when sources support them, fullscreen and progress persistence. Progress is saved periodically and on critical events. Completion is reached at more than 90%.

PROFILES
Up to five profiles per account. Lists, history, progress, recommendations and preferences are isolated by profile.

RECOMMENDATIONS
Initial recommendations use popular/trending and genre similarity. Future versions may use viewing history, completion, list contents, genres, actors, directors and time patterns.

PRIVACY
Minimum data collection. No selling of personal data. Secrets remain server-side.

GOLDEN RULES
Every visible option should work. Routes must be valid. The catalog model must scale. Local/mock data works before external data. Credentials remain private. Documentation remains available. No feature is complete without a test.
